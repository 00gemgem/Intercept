What is Intercept?

Intercept is a novelty panic doll concept: a soft, expressive companion with three playful urgency modes.

Do all modes contact the police?

Every mode opens a confirmation before opening your phone's emergency dialer. Tell the emergency operator you need police. This page cannot dispatch officers or call anyone without your confirmation.

Does it give medical or bathroom advice?

No. Intercept is for humor and companionship, not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.
